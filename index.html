About My Research & Vision

Bridging interpretable machine learning with actionable clinical triage and edge diagnostics

My Story & Objective

As a computer science researcher, I am driven by the imperative that life-critical artificial intelligence must never remain an uninterpretable black box. During my undergraduate studies at AIUB—where I achieved a 3.89 CGPA and four consecutive Dean's List honors—I focused on translating complex gradient boosted trees and hybrid neural architectures into transparent, clinical-grade decision tools.

At ELITE Research Lab LLC, I lead experimental benchmarks on cardiological triage classifiers and multi-tier TreeSHAP attribution audits for peer-reviewed venues. My research contributions span 10 academic papers across IEEE and Springer, focusing on Explainable AI, energy-aware edge diagnostics for chest radiographs, and quantum route feasibility prediction.
